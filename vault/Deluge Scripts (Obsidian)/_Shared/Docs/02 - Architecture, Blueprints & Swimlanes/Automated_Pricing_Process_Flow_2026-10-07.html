1. Trigger & Handshake

PR creation immediately packages line items and issues a REST call to the external pricing engine with SKU, quantities, and technical specifications.

2. Three-Tier Rate Finding

The engine checks active vendor rate cards first, recent PO benchmarks second, and landed parametric formulas third to maximize automated hit rates.

3. Margin Protection

Only pricing configurations meeting minimum profit margins calculate an Approved SRP. Violations fall back to human approval.

4. Instant Progression

Successfully priced items update the PR directly and jump to Ready for PO, removing days of manual back-and-forth.
