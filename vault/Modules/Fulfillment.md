# Fulfillment

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [[Readiness Gate]] | Nothing reaches the pickers until it is received, packaged, assigned and paid for. |
| [[Pick]] | Pickers collect the item from its bin. The picker is not carried on to Pack. |
| [[Pack]] | Name the packer before sending to QC. The packer becomes the Quality Checker. |
| [[QC Checklist]] | Approve routes the item on. Reject needs a reason and raises a Fulfillment exception. |
| [[Crate]] | Nothing moves on until Crating Status is Done. |
| [[Order Aggregation]] | Items that passed QC wait here. Dispatch creates the Service Request in Delivery Management. |
