# Procurement

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [[Inquiry]] | Quote suppliers, clear five readiness conditions, then create the estimate and convert it to a Sales Order. Includes quote groups, the new and edit form, supplier conversations, internal notes and the timeline. |
| [[Purchase Request]] | One PR per item. A PR becomes Ready for PO by itself once four conditions pass, then its Sales Order group is turned into a PO. Includes the Ready queue, PR Groups, the supplier load view, holds and the supplier recommendation. |
| [[Purchase Order]] | Send each PO to its supplier. When the supplier acknowledges, the shipment and receiving are created. |
| [[Shipments]] | Follow each shipment from the supplier to the warehouse door. |
| [[Receiving]] | Count what came off the truck. Damaged or missing goods raise an exception on their own. |
| [[Exceptions]] | Damaged, missing or rejected goods, from both receiving and QC. |
| [[Replacement]] | Approving one creates the replacement shipment. It only completes when the goods are physically received. |
