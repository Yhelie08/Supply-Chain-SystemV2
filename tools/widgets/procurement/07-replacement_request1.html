About Approve RR

Approving is the supplier's own action, done on their portal (Fenteng-RR/ Tony-RR) — they confirm they can fulfill it and fill in Expected Ship Date + ETA in the same step. This aggregate view is read-only for that; "Supplier- Side Status" above reflects their own record's live status. Any Missing_SKUs flagging or new replacement PO now happens automatically the moment this RR is raised, not when the supplier approves — no action here triggers it.

Log Replacement Shipment

Once the supplier has approved, log a new Shipment for the replacement goods — cloned from the original PO's own Shipment (same PO, Customer, Sales Owner). It routes into the SAME Receiving record as the original, no new PO or new RCV created.
