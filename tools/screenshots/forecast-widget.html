A bar counts an order in the week its Likely date falls, and the colour is that order measured against what the client expects (the Sales Order's own target delivery date). The real window is wider than one week — open the Forecast tab for each order's Earliest, Latest, and how many days early or late that lands versus the client's date.

Ang forecast ay isang window (Earliest – Latest), hindi eksaktong petsa. Ang “Client expects” ay ang target delivery date ng Sales Order — galing sa Zoho Books (cf_target_delivery_date) at kinopya sa Fulfillment ng so_fulfillment_sync. Kung wala itong laman, hindi ituturing na on-time ang order — ibubukod ito. Isang bagay lang ang sinusulatan ng widget na ito: ang Forecast Settings na record, sa Forecast Settings na tab. Walang order, PR, shipment o stage record ang ginagalaw nito.
